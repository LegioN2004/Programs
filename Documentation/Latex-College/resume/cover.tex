Dear Hiring Manager,

I am a Computer Science student who enjoys building software, understanding how systems work internally, and learning new technologies by working on real problems. I am excited to apply for the Software Engineer Intern position at EA's Slingshot Studios because the opportunity to work on games used by millions of players combines software engineering with a product where performance, reliability, and user experience all matter.

Through my projects, I have developed a strong foundation in programming, backend development, and systems-oriented engineering. While building Bionexa, a healthcare platform, I worked on RESTful APIs, PostgreSQL, payment processing, inventory workflows, and WebSocket-based real-time communication. This experience taught me how different components of a product interact and how to build features while considering reliability and the overall user experience.

My work on SecretWarden in Rust has taken me further into systems programming. I have worked with ownership and concurrency concepts, asynchronous processes, Unix sockets, inter-process communication, and security boundaries while building a local secret-management daemon. Working on this project has also required me to learn unfamiliar concepts independently and understand how software behaves beyond just the application layer. Alongside Rust, I have experience with C++, Java, JavaScript, and TypeScript, and I continue to strengthen my data structures and algorithms fundamentals.

What particularly interests me about Slingshot Studios is the engineering challenge behind delivering smooth experiences to a large number of players. The focus on building new features, improving performance and load times, participating in code reviews, and working closely with designers and product teams is the kind of environment in which I would like to develop as an engineer. I also enjoy games and am interested in learning more about the technical aspects of game development, including the performance and 3D mathematics involved in creating responsive experiences.

I would be excited to bring my curiosity, programming foundation, and willingness to learn to EA while gaining experience from engineers working on large-scale mobile games and live services. The opportunity to contribute to real game features and learn how a professional game studio approaches engineering would be especially valuable to me.

Enclosed is my resume, which provides further details about my education, projects, and experience. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute to EA's engineering team.
